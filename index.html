Featured  Case

Milestone

One record, four points of view. Students build portfolios and track scholarships, teachers verify work, parents monitor progress, and admins oversee the ecosystem.

Who it serves

Schools, students, and parents. Each role gets its own dashboard so the same achievements and growth data read the right way for the person looking at them.

How it's built

A Next.js and TypeScript interface on Supabase and PostgreSQL, styled with TailwindCSS. Role-based access keeps each dashboard scoped to the people it belongs to.

What it tracks

Scholarships, achievement records, teacher-verified work, and growth analytics across the whole ecosystem.
